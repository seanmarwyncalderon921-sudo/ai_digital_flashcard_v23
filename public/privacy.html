Privacy Policy

Effective/version date: November 19, 2026

This notice explains how the SAPC AI Academic Reviewer project processes personal information when you use accounts, study tools, chat, voice messages, and voice or video calls. The project operator is responsible for deciding why and how this project processes data. If a school formally operates the Service, the school must confirm its role and any required Data Protection Officer contact before deployment.

Information we process

  • Account and profile: Firebase user identifier, email or sign-in provider, username, display name, optional profile photo, bio, grade/strand, and presence/last-seen time.
  • Study activity: saved flashcard decks, course settings, study-session scores and duration, shared-deck content, and app preferences.
  • Chat: conversation membership, message text, replies, reactions, read receipts, timestamps, and files users send. Attachments can include images, documents, video, and voice messages. Other conversation participants can see content sent to them.
  • Calls: call invitations, acceptance/decline and hang-up events, session identifiers, signaling data (including SDP and ICE candidates), and connection/network metadata. The app does not intentionally record call audio or video. WebRTC may expose network addresses to peers and STUN/TURN providers; a TURN relay can carry call media when a peer-to-peer path is unavailable.
  • Safety and requests: policy version acceptance and timestamp, user blocks, abuse reports, and privacy requests.
  • Technical data: request and error logs, IP address, device/browser information available to hosting or connection services, and security/rate-limit events.

Why and on what basis

We use this information to create and secure accounts; provide flashcards, saved progress, chat, calls, and sharing; troubleshoot and prevent abuse; handle user reports and privacy requests; and meet legal obligations. The project relies on the applicable lawful basis for each purpose, which may include consent, steps needed to provide a requested service, legitimate interests in security and abuse prevention, and legal obligations. Consent is not a blanket basis for every processing activity and can be withdrawn where processing relies on consent.

AI processing

When you request AI flashcard generation or subject suggestions, the relevant study text and instructions are sent from the server to Groq’s API using a server-held key. Do not include information about yourself or another person that is not needed for study. Chat content and voice calls are not sent to the AI feature by the app. AI providers may process request data under their own terms and retention settings; consult the provider’s current privacy materials before using sensitive or confidential material.

Service providers and disclosures

  • Firebase Authentication (Google): account sign-in and identity tokens.
  • Supabase: application database and chat attachment storage.
  • Groq: AI flashcard generation and subject detection when requested.
  • Render or the configured application host: serves the app and backend and may process security/connection logs.
  • Configured STUN/TURN services: help WebRTC devices establish or relay calls. The exact provider depends on deployment configuration.
  • Static asset CDNs: the browser loads fonts and client libraries from services including Google Fonts, jsDelivr, and Cloudflare. Those requests expose ordinary connection metadata such as IP address and browser details to the CDN.

These providers may process data in countries outside the Philippines. The project operator should use appropriate processor contracts and safeguards for international transfers. We do not sell personal information. We disclose information only to the relevant service providers, people in a conversation, authorized project operators handling safety/privacy requests, or where law permits or requires it.

Retention and deletion

Saved profiles, decks, study history, messages, and attachments remain in the service until deleted by an available feature or through a verified privacy request. Unsent messages are replaced with an unsent notice and their attachments are removed by the app. Closing a conversation deletes its stored messages and attachments. Call media is not intentionally recorded or retained by the app; signaling is relayed live, while provider security/connection logs follow provider retention. Policy-consent, safety-report, privacy-request, and security records are kept as needed to document consent, review abuse, and meet legal obligations. Deleted data may remain temporarily in provider backups or logs. The project operator should apply a documented schedule and delete it when no longer needed.

Security

The app uses authenticated server APIs, server-held Supabase and AI credentials, access checks, rate limits, and encrypted HTTPS/WSS transport when deployed with valid HTTPS. Chat and call media are transmitted using browser platform security, but no internet service can promise zero risk. Keep credentials private and avoid sending highly sensitive or confidential material.

Your rights and choices

Subject to the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), its Implementing Rules and Regulations, and applicable limits, you may ask whether your information is processed; request access, correction, erasure or blocking; object or withdraw consent where consent is the basis; request portability where applicable; and file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission. The NPC summarizes these data subject rights. You can also remove a message you sent, delete a conversation, or block a user using available chat controls. To request access, correction, deletion, or withdrawal, submit the form below while signed in. We may ask for reasonable verification and will respond through the project’s request-handling process. Withdrawal does not undo processing already lawfully performed and may prevent use of features that need the data.
